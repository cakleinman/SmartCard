# Audio attribution

The Army Song (`The Army Goes Rolling Along`) bundled into `index.html` is in
the public domain.

- Source: [Creazilla — The Army Goes Rolling Along](https://creazilla.com/media/audio/7955737/the-army-goes-rolling-along)
- License: Public Domain
- Re-encoded to 80 kbps mono MP3 to keep the single-file app under 2 MB

The Soldier's Creed audio slot is intentionally left empty in this build. To
add one:
- Run `./bundle-audio.sh path/to/creed.mp3` (you'll need to provide your own MP3
  if the default Internet Archive URL is unreachable from your network), or
- Use Settings → Bundled recordings → Import on the phone where you'll study.

The U.S. Army Band's recordings are public domain under 17 U.S.C. § 105.
